The canvas belonged to the museum in Magdeburg when it was moved to a salt mine for safekeeping during the Second World War. Its trail goes cold in 1945; it is generally considered destroyed.

Reproductions made before its disappearance still allow us to encounter this painter on his way. The exact fate of the original remains uncertain.
